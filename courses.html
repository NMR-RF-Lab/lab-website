Courses

Selected courses

Taught through Biomedical Engineering and Electrical & Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University.

BMEN

Biomedical Engineering

  • BMEN 420

    Introduction to Medical Imaging

    A signals and systems approach to all major medical imaging modalities, including Projection Radiography, CT, Nuclear Medicine (Planar Scintigraphy, PET, SPECT), Ultrasound, and MRI.

  • BMEN 427/627

    Magnetic Resonance Engineering Lab

    A unique hands-on course in which the student builds an entire desktop MR spectrometer, including RF coils, gradients, switching, and LabVIEW-based interface and front-end control. Crosslisted with ECEN 463/763.

  • BMEN 428/628

    Biomedical Electromagnetics

    Classical electromagnetics from DC to RF, taught via biomedical applications.
