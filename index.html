Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Radiofrequency Lab

The NMR RF Lab functions as part of the Magnetic Resonance Systems Lab at Texas A&M University. Our research focuses on the development of hardware and methodologies to address challenges in the field of magnetic resonance imaging and spectroscopy.

  • 1 T20 cm Samsung extremity scanner
  • 4.7 T33 cm scanner, Varian Unity/Inova
  • 4.7 T40 cm scanner with RF screen room
  • 7 TPhilips Achieva at UT Southwestern
Research focus

Hardware and methods for MR imaging and spectroscopy

Facilities, projects and publications
  • Novel RF coil design for high-field imaging and spectroscopy
  • Technology to increase the sensitivity of non-hydrogen detection at low fields and clinical fields
  • Detection and analysis of NMR-based biomarkers of disease
  • Transmit and receive parallel imaging coils and methodology
    • Small animal imaging
    • Non-periodic and one-time events
    • High-field human applications
  • Full wave modeling and simulations
